# Spaghetti AI Demo Script

Target length: 60–120 seconds.

## 0–10 seconds

“Searching YouTube for a technical topic usually gives you entire videos, not a learning sequence. Spaghetti AI builds a prerequisite-aware path and finds the exact parts worth watching.”

Show the homepage and enter a technical topic.

## 10–30 seconds

While generation runs:

“Behind the interface, the system decomposes the topic into concepts, builds prerequisite relationships, searches educational videos, extracts transcripts, creates timestamped chunks, and uses semantic retrieval to find candidate explanations.”

## 30–60 seconds

Show the generated learning sequence.

“Each concept is placed in a recommended learning order. Spaghetti selects transcript-grounded segments rather than entire videos.”

Scroll to a resource.

“The learner can see what the selected clip teaches before opening it, along with a recommended watch window that includes a few seconds of context.”

## 60–85 seconds

Open “Why this clip?”

“This panel exposes engineering evidence such as educational quality, semantic relevance, the selection threshold, alternatives considered, and transcript verification.”

## 85–105 seconds

Click “Watch with context.”

“The link opens YouTube close to the selected explanation, with a short context buffer so the learner does not land in the middle of a sentence.”

## 105–120 seconds

“Spaghetti AI also validates concept coverage, transcript grounding, and duplicate resources, and uses persistent caching and rate limits to make the deployed system more reliable and cost controlled.”
